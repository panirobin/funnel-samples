## Audience
- **Fears:** the expensive first mistake (overpaying, bad substance, wrong location); the bank saying no; not enough Eigenkapital; notary/contract intimidation — "once you've done it once you lose the fear of notaries" (DAS INVESTMENT interview, female investors)
- **Desires:** financial independence as a woman; Altersvorsorge secured; monthly cashflow covering fixed costs
- **Objections:** too little equity; no time next to job/kids (Sophia is a mother of three); "prices/interest are too high right now"; "I'm not a finance person"
- **Triggers:** ETF depot "läuft" and the question „Was kommt als Nächstes?"; seeing peers buy
- **Typical beginner mistakes** (myinvest24, steier-immobilien): emotional purchase, too little equity, ignoring location, doing it all alone
