Why your top seller is quietly eating your cash

// The difference

What changes after these 60 minutes

Most resellers under 10k aren't short of deals. They're short of cash — because it's sitting in stock that turns once every six months.

Without the Cash Turn maths

  • Seller Central says 8k in sales this month. Your bank account says something else entirely.
  • Anything that clears 50% ROI goes in the basket — even if it takes six months to sell through.
  • You keep reordering the SKU with a 9% refund rate because it's your "best seller".
  • Every free evening goes on sourcing, and on Monday the cash is still stuck in Amazon's warehouse.
  • So you buy another leads list to fix it — and more money goes into more slow stock.

With the Cash Turn maths

  • You know your net profit per SKU, and it matches what actually lands in the bank.
  • You buy for turns: 30% that sells in a month beats 50% that sells in six.
  • High-refund SKUs get cut, and the lost and damaged stock gets claimed back.
  • The same capital goes round three times a quarter instead of once — without extra sourcing hours.
  • Fewer, faster deals from suppliers you already buy from. More profit, not more stock.
