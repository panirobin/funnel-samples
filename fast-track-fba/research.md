## Audience

Who: UK and US Amazon resellers, online arbitrage first, some moving into wholesale. Side hustle that's become serious — "doing it on maybe a Saturday afternoon when they've got a few hours" (Eamon Kennedy, on Tom's channel).

### Fears
- **Shared leads tank the price.** "Too many sellers on the listing… everyone want to win the Buybox… tons of naïve newbies sellers" — Trustpilot, Arbitrage Hero 1★ (via WebFetch summary — close paraphrase). "Price tanking" when lists go to many people at once — OAHunt.
- **Paying for sourcing that finds nothing.** "VAs couldn't find even a few leads during an 8-hour shift" — Trustpilot, Fast Track FBA 1★ (Ayaan), summarised.
- **Gated/IP trouble.** "They were gated in too many of the products" — OAHunt, lead-list expectations article.
- **Money stuck in Amazon.** Tom's own framing: capital is the limit, stock sitting 6–12 months.

### Desires
- Net profit they can see, not revenue (Tom's content; Seller comment "The business is about making money not winning the box").
- Get past 10k/month — his coaching headline.
- Time back: "they think about VAs… where do I go? What do I do?" — Xp9sifusW2E.

### Objections
- "Lists and leads services are a scam once 20 people buy the same deal." (Arbitrage Hero reviews)
- "Not enough good selling deals to make actual money." (review snippet, search result)
- "I've only got a few hours a week / small capital."
- "Is OA dead?" (Seller Assistant blog title "Is Online Arbitrage Dead for New Sellers in 2025?")

### Buying triggers
- Q4 approaching (he's run Q4 webinars before — guest webinar with Luke Dugan, 2019).
- A payout that's smaller than expected despite "good" sales.
- Buy Box changes (localised offers) — current, he's posting about it.
- A price tank on a lead they bought.
