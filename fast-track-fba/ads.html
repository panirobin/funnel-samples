Hook

If your Amazon payout never matches your sales report, you're probably leaking money in three places. And Amazon isn't going to tell you about any of them.

Body

Number one: refunds. In my business, anything above a four percent refund rate, I want to know why — and most of the time I just won't rebuy it. You lose the sale, and you still pay the FBA fee.

Number two: lost and damaged stock. Amazon moves your stock around their warehouses — things get lost, things get broken. That's your stock. You should be claiming it back.

Number three: returns where the customer gets their money and the item never comes back. You're left holding the bag.
