## Audience

Who: employees with a steady salary (~2.000–3.500 € netto), late 20s–40s, some savings
on the Girokonto or in ETFs, follow finance content, have decided Immobilien interest
them, haven't bought. Goal is often modest: Voll- → Teilzeit, not Lambo.

- **Fears:** buying the wrong flat (overpriced, bad Lage), Sonderumlage / Reparaturen /
  Leerstand not in the Kalkulation (clean-invoice checklist, test.de „grobe Schnitzer");
  bank advice that's one-sided (test.de Finanztest: Berater informieren unzureichend,
  riskante Vollfinanzierungen); being overwhelmed by Finanzierung, Notar, Objektsuche
  (business-punk guest piece).
- **Desires:** passives Einkommen from Mieteinnahmen; Voll- → Teilzeit; protection
  against Inflation; not relying on the staatliche Rente; something for the family
  „über Generationen".
- **Objections:** „Jetzt ist ein schlechter Zeitpunkt" (he answers: „eine der besten
  Phasen zum Kaufen, während 90 % aussteigen"); „ETFs sind einfacher" (he does ETFs too);
  „Ich habe zu wenig Eigenkapital / Einkommen"; „Ich hab keine Zeit neben dem Job";
  „Coaches verkaufen nur Kurse".
- **Stuck moment:** endless research without buying — the plan becomes an
  „Endlosschleife" (von-poll blog); Exposés get saved and never durchgerechnet, or
  durchgerechnet and found „viel zu teuer" — then nothing happens.
- **Triggers:** salary raise that doesn't move Vermögen; Rentenbescheid; money idle on
  the Girokonto; friends buying.
