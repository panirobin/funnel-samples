## Audience

Reddit/forums unreachable (memory: Reddit blocked); WebSearch returned guides, not first-hand threads. Audience voice therefore from his FAQ objections + bank-side sources.

- **Fears:** no Eigenkapital; bank says no; buying a "Leiche" (profit inflated, Kasse leergeräumt); no Branchenerfahrung; being burned as a buyer in the first talk; "traust dich aber nicht". (site FAQ, captions)
- **Desires:** be Unternehmer from day 1, customers + team + Umsatz from day 1, no 70-hour founding years, Freiheit. (site)
- **Objections:** "Ist das seriös oder nur ein weiteres Marketing-Produkt?"; "Was, wenn ich keine Branchenerfahrung habe?"; "Brauche ich Eigenkapital?" (site FAQ)
- **Bank view:** banks test the whole succession concept; unrealistic price expectations, underestimated financing need, missing collateral/equity surface here; solvable via contract design, Förderkredite, Verkäuferdarlehen. (Bankenverband brochure via sh.ermoeglicher.de; sevdesk guide)
- **Triggers:** succession wave — KfW: ~560.000 SME owners plan succession by end of 2025, about half without a suitable successor (cited on his site, KfW Fokus Nr. 421).
