// The mechanism

What a webinar funnel actually does

Entrepreneurs don't book a call from a cold page. They book after you've run the diagnosis on them live and shown how you think — which is exactly what the Performance Analyse call needs walking in.

Step 01

Traffic goes to one page

Instead of asking strangers to buy, you ask them to show up to something free and specific. The commitment is small, so the cost per registration stays low.

Step 02

The webinar does the selling

You run the absence test and the "the ceiling is a self-image" reframe live. By the end, the people who want help have felt the diagnosis — so booking the Analyse call is the obvious next step, not a pitch.

Step 03

The funnel pays for itself early

The €49 Performance-Paket runs immediately after registration, so a share of ad spend comes back before the webinar even airs. That's what makes filling the room repeatable.
