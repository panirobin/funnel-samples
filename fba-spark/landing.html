// The difference

What changes after these 55 minutes

The problem was never that you can't read a Keepa chart. It's that nobody gave you the order to read it in — so every listing gets the same twenty minutes, including the ones that were never going to work.

Sourcing without a system

  • You open every listing that looks profitable, and most of the session goes into ones you'll never buy.
  • You hunt coupons for an hour on a lead that was never close enough at base price to get there.
  • You buy on today's Buy Box price, then the offer count doubles and your margin goes with it.
  • You find out the brand was on the listing the day the complaint lands in your inbox.
  • Every week starts from zero, because nothing you bought last month was worth buying twice.

Sourcing with the scan

  • You glance at a chart, see a kill signal, and move on before you've spent a minute on it.
  • You only go coupon-hunting on leads that already clear the bar at list price.
  • You price against where the listing actually peaks, not where it happens to sit this afternoon.
  • The listings most likely to get you complained about are gone before you ever open them.
  • Good leads get saved and tracked, so next month is reorders instead of another hunt.
