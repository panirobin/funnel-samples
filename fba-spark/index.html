// The mechanism

Where this sits in what you already run

Right now the path is a YouTube teardown → a DM → the Strategy Call at fbaspark.com. The workshop slots in as the warm step before that call — a room where people watch you source live, so the call is a decision rather than a first impression.

Step 01

Your video traffic gets one clear next step

Instead of hoping a viewer DMs you, you invite them to something free, specific and dated. Small commitment, low cost per registration, and it scales past the DM inbox.

Step 02

The workshop does the selling

You share your screen for 55 minutes and kill listings live. By the end, the people who want you sourcing with them have already seen exactly how you work — so the Strategy Call books itself instead of being pitched.

Step 03

The Vault offer recovers ad spend early

The one-time Lead Vault runs right after registration, so a share of spend comes back before the event even airs. That's what lets you actually pay for traffic instead of relying on the algorithm.
