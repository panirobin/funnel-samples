// The mechanism

Where this sits in what you already run

Right now the path is Accent Archetype Quiz → free Skool community → a 1:1 Accent Strategy Call. The workshop slots in as the warm step before that call — a room where people watch you fix a sound live, so the call is a decision, not a first impression.

Step 01

Quiz takers get one clear next step

Instead of hoping quiz traffic wanders into Skool, you invite them to something free, specific and dated. Small commitment, low cost per registration.

Step 02

The workshop does the selling

You run live simulations for 52 minutes. By the end, the people who want more have seen exactly how you work — so the Strategy Call books itself instead of being pitched.

Step 03

The Vault offer recovers ad spend early

The one-time Simulation Vault runs right after registration, so a share of spend comes back before the event even airs. That's what lets you actually pay for traffic.
